# DECLASSIFIED MEDIA COMPANY — org structure (lucky's order 2026-10-18)

The channel is run like a real company. Every department has named employees
(cron jobs + agents), KPIs, and a work-or-die accountability rule: a missed
run or an unverified claim is a defect, logged and fixed the same day.

## Departments

### 1. RESEARCH DESK (facts or death)
- **Employees:** daily factory (research phase), yt-trend-scout-daily
- **Job:** every script verified against 2+ public sources BEFORE production.
  Sources logged in research-notes.txt. No source = no video. A single
  unverified fact = full rebuild.
- **KPI:** 100% of published shorts with 2+ logged sources. Zero retractions.

### 2. PRODUCTION HOUSE (the bar)
- **Employees:** daily factory (build phase), local-3d-reel-factory skill
- **Job:** 5-layer build per RAAZ-PRODUCTION-PLAN.md — real images first,
  10–12 shots, cut every 3–6s, MiDaS parallax, ElevenLabs VO, 3D captions,
  RAAZ theme. Zero-mistake QA gate before anything ships.
- **KPI:** every short passes all 7 QA checks; 6+ frames viewed per short.

### 3. PUBLISHING & DISTRIBUTION
- **Employees:** daily factory (publish phase)
- **Job:** YouTube Shorts auto-upload daily ~06:07 IST (US prime evening).
  Instagram draft queued for lucky's one-tap approval — never auto-published
  (his rule; runtime safeguard).
- **KPI:** 7 shorts/week on YouTube, 7 drafts/week queued for IG. Zero missed days.

### 4. GROWTH & ANALYTICS
- **Employees:** yt-analytics-pull (daily), yt-viral-watchdog, yt-weekly-report, yt-monthly-earnings
- **Job:** track subs, views, watch time, top videos. Any video 3x above
  channel median → dissect the formula → feed it to the Research Desk.
  Weekly growth report, monthly earnings accounting.
- **KPI:** subscriber count reported every run; formula replications attempted
  within 48h of a breakout.

### 5. MONETIZATION OFFICE (real money, honest numbers)
- **Employees:** yt-monthly-earnings, factory (milestone checks)
- **Job:** drive the channel through the money stages and report REAL numbers:
  - 500 subs → enable CHANNEL MEMBERSHIPS (member-only goal) the same day
  - 1k subs + 10M Shorts views/90d → YPP application the same day
  - ~10k engaged subs → media kit + sponsor outreach (needs his business email)
  - Long-form weekly documentary (Stage 4) → $8–20 CPM engine
- **KPI:** no missed unlock — the day a threshold is hit, the money switch is flipped.

### 6. TREASURY (his $0 rule)
- **Employees:** yt-budget-watchdog, earn-heartbeat-watchdog, ledger-watchdog
- **Job:** RunPod $3.74 lasts 15 days (~$0.25/day) — GPU in short bursts only,
  pod terminated after every use. ElevenLabs cached, never regenerated.
  Every rupee/dollar logged. His investment stays $0 — always.
- **KPI:** daily spend ≤ $0.25; any breach reported same day.

## Accountability — WORK OR DIE

- Every employee (cron) reports through its final message. Silence = defect.
- A failed run is retried once after fixing the cause; a second failure
  escalates to lucky with the honest reason — never hidden.
- No fake numbers anywhere: views, subs, revenue reported exactly as the
  APIs return them. Inflating a metric = the one firing offense.
- The boss's taps (§8 of the master plan) are the ONLY thing the company
  waits for. Everything else runs without asking.

## Command

lucky = Chairman. Sets targets, owns the taps, kills anything.
Ultron = CEO. Runs all six departments, 24/7, reports verdict-first.
